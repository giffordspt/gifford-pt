ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Jack.

I've been training for over 10 years, and throughout that time I've developed a real passion for boxing, strength training and understanding what the human body is capable of.

My experience includes coaching CrossFit and boxing, working with clients internationally, as well as training in the Royal Marines. But what I've come to appreciate most is that good training isn't just about pushing yourself harder — it's about enjoying the process and making progress that lasts.

I believe fitness should be something that adds to your life, not something you dread doing. Whether you're learning to box, getting stronger or simply building confidence in yourself, I want you to feel comfortable, challenged and excited to improve.

Some sessions will be tough, others will focus on learning new skills or improving how you move. The important thing is finding a balance that works for you.

My goal is to help you enjoy training, understand your body and become more capable, both inside and outside the gym.

Train with purpose. Enjoy the process. Build for life.
