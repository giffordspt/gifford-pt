ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Jack.

I'm a personal trainer and boxing coach. I run 1:1 personal training, boxing, small group classes and online programmes for people who want to feel genuinely better, stronger, calmer and more capable.

I care far more about how you feel than about numbers on a scale. My job is to meet you where you are, keep it sustainable, and help you build the kind of resilience that carries into the rest of your life.
