-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  FIRST, THE IMPORTANT BIT ABOUT DATA SAFETY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Your website is a "static" site — just pages (text, images, styling). It has:
    - NO database
    - NO client logins
    - NO stored customer records

So there is NO pot of client data sitting on the website for anyone to steal.
If someone broke into the hosting, the worst case is they mess with the page
text — there's no customer list there to take, because one doesn't exist.

Your clients' actual data lives with specialist companies whose whole job is to
keep it safe:

  * BOOKINGS  -> Google Calendar (bookings land straight in your calendar with
                                  the client's name, email and chosen time)
  * PAYMENTS  -> Stripe / PayPal (card details — handled entirely by them;
                                  the data never touches your website and you
                                  never see or store card numbers yourself)
  * ENQUIRIES / REVIEWS -> your email + Instagram

That's a deliberately safe design. It means your security job is simple:
protect your ACCOUNTS. The real-world risk for a small business is not a
Hollywood "website hack" — it's someone tricking you into giving up a password
(phishing). Guard against that and you've covered the big risks.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  YOUR SECURITY CHECKLIST  (do these once, then relax)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ] 1. Turn on TWO-FACTOR (2FA / two-step login) on EVERY account:
        - Website host (Netlify/Cloudflare)
        - Domain registrar (where you bought giffordpt.com/.co.uk)
        - Google account (your calendar / bookings live here)
        - Stripe / PayPal
        - Your email  <-- most important; it can reset all the others
        - Instagram
     2FA means even if someone gets your password, they still can't get in.

[ ] 2. Use a PASSWORD MANAGER (e.g. Bitwarden - free, or the one built into
        your phone/browser). Give every account a different strong password.
        Never reuse the same password across sites.

[ ] 3. Make sure the site shows HTTPS (a padlock in the browser bar). This is
        automatic and free on Netlify/Cloudflare — it encrypts everything sent
        between visitors and the site. Don't take payments on anything without it.

[ ] 4. Turn on "domain lock" / "registrar lock" at your domain registrar, and
        2FA there. This stops anyone hijacking your domain name.

[ ] 5. Collect only what you need. In Calendly, don't add extra questions asking
        for sensitive info you don't require. Less data collected = less to worry
        about.

[ ] 6. Don't keep loose copies of client lists in random places (old emails,
        unprotected spreadsheets, WhatsApp). Let Calendly be the record.

[ ] 7. Be suspicious of emails/texts asking you to "log in" or "confirm your
        payment details" via a link. Go to the site directly instead. This is
        how most small businesses actually get compromised.

[ ] 8. (UK/GDPR) Because Calendly and Stripe process your clients' personal
        data, you should have a short PRIVACY POLICY on the site saying what you
        collect and who processes it. Ask me and I'll draft a starter one for you.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  MANAGING THE SITE DAY-TO-DAY  (easier than you'd think)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

There are no servers to babysit, no software to update, no database to back up.
A static site is about the lowest-maintenance thing there is. Here's what you'd
actually do:

CHANGE WORDS ON THE PAGE (services, bio, FAQ answers, timetable):
  1. Open the file  gifford-pt/index.html  in any text editor (even Notepad,
     though "VS Code" - free - is nicer and colour-codes things).
  2. Search for the pencil marks  EDIT  to find every changeable bit, OR just
     find the text you want to change.
  3. Type your new text between the tags, e.g.
        <h3>Boxing</h3>   ->   <h3>Boxing & Pad Work</h3>
     Don't touch the < > tags themselves, just the words between them.
  4. Save the file.
  5. Put it live (see "PUBLISHING CHANGES" below).

ADD A REVIEW:
  In index.html, find the Reviews section, copy one whole
  <blockquote class="card review"> ... </blockquote> block, paste it below the
  others, and edit the quote + name. Save & publish. (You add these by hand on
  purpose, so no trolls can spam fake ones.)

CHANGE CLASS TIMES:
  In index.html, find the timetable and edit the day blocks. Save & publish.

CHANGE BOOKING TIMES OR PRICES:
  You do this inside GOOGLE CALENDAR (your appointment schedule) — NOT in the
  website files. It instantly updates what clients can book. No code needed.

ADD JACK'S PHOTO:
  Drop a photo named  jack.jpg  into the  gifford-pt/assets/  folder, publish.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PUBLISHING CHANGES (getting edits onto the live site)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Whichever host you chose (see HOSTING-AND-DOMAINS.txt):

  NETLIFY (drag-and-drop) — easiest:
     Go to your site in the Netlify dashboard -> "Deploys" -> drag the whole
     gifford-pt folder onto the upload area. Live in ~10 seconds.

  NETLIFY / CLOUDFLARE via GitHub — tidier long-term:
     If the site is connected to a GitHub repo, you edit the files, "commit"
     the change, and it republishes itself automatically. This also keeps a
     history so you can undo mistakes. (A bit more to learn up front.)

Tip: after publishing, do a hard refresh (Ctrl+F5) if you don't see the change
straight away — that's just your browser showing a cached copy.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  WHAT YOU CAN DO YOURSELF vs WHEN TO ASK FOR HELP
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Comfortable to do yourself once set up:
  - Edit text, prices (in Calendly), timetable, add reviews, swap photos.
  - Publish updates (drag-and-drop).

Worth asking for help with (one-off or occasional):
  - First-time setup of hosting, domain and Calendly+payments.
  - Adding a new SECTION or changing the design/layout.
  - Writing the privacy policy.
  - Anything where you'd be editing the styling (CSS) or scripts (JS).

You don't need to become a web developer to run this. If you can edit a document
and drag a folder, you can keep this site up to date.
