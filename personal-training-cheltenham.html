Personal training that works for real life

There is no one-size-fits-all workout. Whether you want to get stronger, improve your general fitness, feel more capable in the gym or prepare for a physical challenge, your sessions start with where you are now.

Coaching can include strength training, kettlebells, bodyweight movements, conditioning and mobility. Jack explains technique, adjusts the difficulty and helps you work towards consistent progress without making training needlessly intimidating.

What happens in your sessions?

Start with a free introductory taster so you can talk through your goals, ask questions and try some training. Together, you can decide whether one, two or three coached sessions per week fits your routine.

During your regular sessions you'll focus on good movement, exercises selected for your ability and gradually progressing over time. If you enjoy boxing as well as weights, you can ask about combining the two.

Who is it for?

Beginners are welcome. So are people returning to exercise, looking to build strength and confidence, or wanting more purposeful training for sport and fitness assessments. Coaching is adapted to the individual rather than expecting everyone to train the same way.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know before you start

Do I need experience in the gym?

No. Your starting point shapes the training. Jack can teach you exercise technique and gradually build from there.

Can I mix strength training with boxing?

Yes. If you'd enjoy some non-contact boxing padwork alongside strength and conditioning, discuss that with Jack when booking.

Where does personal training take place?

Sessions can be arranged at the training location at 4 King Alfred Way, Cheltenham, GL52 6QP. Ask Jack about the venue when confirming your session.
